About Campus Food Waste Project

This project aims to reduce food wastage on campus using technology.

Students can donate surplus food, check current supply, and receive intelligent AI predictions for shortages.

Google Firebase ensures real-time updates, and a rule-based AI predicts food shortage risk automatically.
